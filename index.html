Our Loan Process

From application to funded

We originate, review, underwrite, fund and service our own loans, so decisions happen in house.

1. Apply online

Fill out our easy loan application. A loan officer reviews it and calls you about anything else we need.

2. Send your documents

Submit your purchase contract and renovation list. We send you a Good Faith Estimate to review and approve.

3. Processing and underwriting

We order the appraisal and title. Our construction manager visits the property with you to go over the bid sheet.

4. Close and fund

We set up the closing with our preferred attorney and wire the funds. You provide the property insurance.
